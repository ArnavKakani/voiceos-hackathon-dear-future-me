# DFM Voice — Demo Video Shot List

Two speakers: **A = builder (phone in hand)**, **B = friend (filming + foil)**.
Route: Dublin/Pleasanton BART → Market St → Blue Bottle on Market → Frontier Tower.
16 shots, 2–3 sec each ≈ 35–40 sec cut. Film everything twice; grab 5 sec of
B-roll (train passing, escalator, Market foot traffic, coffee pour) for cutaways.

Pre-flight: phone charged, DFM + Claude keys configured, demo account seeded with
2–3 past entries, widget on Lock Screen, ChatGPT custom GPT convo pre-scrolled,
web notebook logged in on B's phone. Rehearse the two live voice lines — STT is
the only unscripted variable.
